About me

I'm a B.Tech graduate in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (DMIHER University) who grew up on Linux. I bridge two worlds: deep, hands-on Red Hat system administration and IEEE-published deep-learning research.

On the ops side I manage RHEL 9 servers — storage, SELinux, firewalld, SSH hardening — automate everything with Ansible, and containerize with Docker. On the research side I've published 6 peer-reviewed papers on CNN-based medical imaging, YOLOv8 edge-AI surveillance, blockchain and IoT in healthcare.

I'm Red Hat Academy certified (RH124, RH294, DO180) and currently open to Linux administration, DevOps and AI research roles.
